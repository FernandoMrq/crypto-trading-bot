## Slice `strategy-contract`

**Objective**

Define the minimum strategy vocabulary for spot day trading: market snapshot, signal, confidence, desired position, order intent, quote id, position, portfolio, fees, slippage, and time window. Include strategy version and configuration hash so historical decisions remain reproducible. The first bounded universe is allowlisted liquid spot markets, with momentum/breakout and mean-reversion baselines. Leverage, derivatives, unlimited token discovery, and autonomous every-new-token sniping are excluded until separately evidenced.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Every order intent states why it exists, strategy version, input snapshot id, expiry, max spend, min receive, and risk budget.
- Buy/sell/transfer are distinct; a transfer cannot be generated by a price strategy.
- Position accounting supports EVM tokens, BTC UTXO quantities, Lightning balances, and CEX balances without pretending they are interchangeable.
- Strategy configuration validation rejects ambiguous units/timeframes and unsafe defaults.
- Strategy configuration declares signal cadence, holding horizon, maximum concurrent positions, and whether an advisory AI may provide context; it never grants AI execution authority.

## Slice `risk-engine`

**Objective**

Implement pre-trade and portfolio limits: max order notional, daily loss, per-token exposure, wallet/account concentration, gas reserve, price impact, slippage, quote age, token-risk score, bridge risk, CEX permission, rate limits, and emergency stop. Risk decisions are immutable evidence attached to execution intents.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Missing or stale data produces `Reject` or `ManualReview`, never an implicit approval.
- Every limit is evaluated in the correct asset/base currency with explicit price provenance.
- Emergency stop blocks new broadcasts but allows reconciliation and safe reads.
- Risk rules are versioned and replayable.
- Tests cover boundary values, decimal precision, stale quotes, provider disagreement, and concurrent exposure reservation.

## Slice `market-data`

**Objective**

Normalize provider outputs into timestamped, source-tagged market snapshots. Separate indicative price from executable quote. Include Binance Spot/Futures market type, interval, candle/trade sequence, oscillation/volatility features, liquidity, route, fee, gas, block height, CEX order-book timestamp, and provider health. Remove shared mutable adapter state and check HTTP status/content before parsing.

**Acceptance criteria**

- A stale or contradictory market snapshot cannot feed a live order.
- Provider rate limits use typed clients, cancellation, bounded retries, and backoff.
- On-chain data can be replayed from block/transaction evidence; CEX data can be replayed from recorded fixtures.
- Token decimals and base/quote units are explicit.
- Binance oscillation data has reproducible raw/normalized versions, gap detection, checksum/provenance, and chronological train/validation/test boundaries.
- Provider/API credentials are resolved through the secure store and redacted.

## Slice `backtest-engine`

**Objective**

Add a deterministic replay engine. Evaluate QuantConnect LEAN as an optional strategy/backtest integration, but keep the domain event/replay contract internal so it can run without the external engine. Model gas, LP fees, aggregator/bridge fees, slippage, liquidity/price impact, latency, partial fills, failed transactions, unavailable liquidity, and the latency/abstention behavior of the AI sibling plan. A backtest must compare no-AI, numerical-ML, and LLM-advisory variants when those variants are enabled.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Same input snapshot/event stream, strategy version, and seed produce identical results.
- Backtests cannot use live signing or live account secrets.
- Results include equity curve, drawdown, turnover, fees, gas, failed operations, exposure, and sensitivity to slippage.
- A strategy that passes only on indicative price but fails executable quote/fee modeling is rejected by the report.
- Any AI-assisted result includes model version, feature/data snapshot, advisory latency, abstention rate, and a comparison against the deterministic baseline.
- Golden datasets cover an EVM swap, BTC UTXO spend, Lightning payment, and CEX order where available.

## Slice `paper-trading`

**Objective**

Run the real quote/risk/queue/reconciliation pipeline with a paper execution adapter that records the transaction intent and simulated fill but never signs or broadcasts. Shadow mode may compare a real quote with paper result, but all live provider inputs are stored with provenance.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Paper mode cannot construct a production signer or call a broadcast method; this is enforced by DI and tests.
- Paper fills model quote expiry, slippage, fees, gas, partial fill, and provider failure.
- UI shows paper/live mode prominently and persists it in every operation.
- Paper results can be compared with backtest results and exported for review.

## Slice `strategy-gate`

**Objective**

Produce a live-enablement report and block live execution until strategy, risk, custody, provider, worker recovery, testnet/regtest evidence, and AI evidence are present when AI influence is enabled. The application should support operator-selected strategies and connectors, but not claim profitable automation.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Required evidence includes a minimum paper period, backtest datasets, drawdown/limit results, operational alerts, recovery drills, and signed-off risk parameters.
- If AI is enabled, required evidence also includes held-out temporal evaluation, model/version hash, schema-validity rate, abstention quality, p50/p95 latency, drift thresholds, rollback to no-AI mode, and proof that the LLM cannot sign or broadcast.
- Live enablement is an audited configuration change and can be revoked by emergency stop.
- README and UI distinguish implemented capability from planned/experimental capability.
- No plan or test implies financial return or guaranteed execution.

## Technology rationale

The repository currently has no defined strategy, risk policy, or valid backtest. LEAN is a candidate for a mature C#/Python backtest surface, but it must not replace the bot's own deterministic event and fee model. The first product gate is reproducibility and controlled execution, not a promise of profit.
